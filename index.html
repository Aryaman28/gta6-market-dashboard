📊 SCALE OF THE LAUNCH
At $3.79B day-one, GTA VI surpassed GTA V's inflation-adjusted ~$0.95B by roughly 4×. The $80 price point (↑33% vs GTA5's $60) and simultaneous PS5/Xbox/PC launch drove the step-change. Year-1 analyst consensus: $3.77B — Wedbush bull case reaches $4.2B. At TTWO's $39.3B market cap, that's ~9.4× year-1 revenue, supported by GTA Online recurring tail.
