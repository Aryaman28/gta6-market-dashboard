📊 Scale of the Launch
At $3.79B day-one, GTA VI surpassed GTA V's inflation-adjusted ~$0.95B day-one by roughly 4× . The $80 price point (vs. GTA5's $60 — a 33% increase) and simultaneous PS5/Xbox/PC launch drove the step-change. Year-1 analyst consensus sits at $3.4–4.2B; Wedbush's $4.2B bull case implies TTWO's $39.3B market cap at ~9.4× year-1 revenue — rich, but supported by GTA Online recurring revenue tail.

Digital-Only: Winners & Losers
GTA VI launched as digital-only (no physical disc). GameStop CEO publicly criticised the format, calling it harmful to consumers — yet GME stock still rose ~5% on the launch window (Nov 17→20) driven by accessories and trading-in of old hardware. Physical retail (Best Buy, Target) faces long-term headwinds; Q3 earnings on Nov 24 will be the first read on hardware bundle sales.
